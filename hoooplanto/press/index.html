About the game

Short: Toss rings, dive the deep sea, discover 85 real ocean facts and clean up litter.

Hoooplanto is a relaxing ring toss game set in the deep ocean. Players seat glowing rings on their pegs with a single hold-and-release, and follow Lanto, a small glowing anglerfish, from the sunlit shallows to the bottom of the Mariana Trench.

On the way down they find 85 Deep Notes: real, checked facts about sea life, the deep sea and ocean pollution, each placed at its true depth. They catch drifting plastic bags, bottles and ghost nets with their rings, and keep a field guide card for every animal and shipwreck they discover, with its IUCN Red List status where one is confirmed.

A cast of seven sea creatures keeps the player company, each with a voice and a personality, and stars in a weekly animated episode on social media. Each episode comes with an in-game event and a ring that can only be won that week.

Features

  • One-touch timing: hold to charge, release to land the ring.
  • 1,094 levels across five ocean zones, with clocks, drifting rings and currents.
  • 85 Deep Notes and a field guide of 29 animals and 8 shipwrecks.
  • Weekly events tied to a new episode every Friday.
  • A free daily challenge level that never costs a life.
  • Cloud save with Google Play Games, and 16 languages.

A note on the ocean facts

Every Deep Note is a real, checked fact, and each one is found at the depth where it belongs. The in-game litter clean-up is a game counter, not a real-world clean-up; the tips the crew shares, like carrying a reusable bottle and refusing plastic bags, are real.
