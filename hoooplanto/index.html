Meet the crew

How it plays

Hold, let go, land it

Charge a ring and release it at the right moment to seat it on its peg. Clear a level without a miss for 3 stars.

Clean up the sea

Catch drifting bags, bottles and ghost nets with your rings, and watch the litter clear from your map zone by zone.

A new event every week

A weekly goal, a new episode with the crew, and a ring you can only win that week.

1,094 levels deep

One level for every 10 metres, through five ocean zones, down to the bottom of the Mariana Trench.

Screenshots

Real facts, found at their real depth

Every Deep Note in the game is a checked fact about sea life, the deep sea or ocean pollution, placed at the depth where it belongs. The Aquarium keeps a field guide card for every animal and shipwreck you find, with its IUCN Red List status where one is confirmed.

The litter is a game counter, not a real clean-up. The tips Lanto shares, like carrying a reusable bottle, are real.
