Hoooplanto Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers Hoooplanto from the Samsung Galaxy Store and HUAWEI AppGallery.

What we store

Your progress, settings and shells balance are stored on this device. We (the Hoooplanto developers) don’t run any server that collects your data.

Ads

Hoooplanto shows ads through AppLovin MAX: optional video ads you can watch for rewards, and occasional ads between levels. To show and measure ads, AppLovin and the ad networks it works with may collect your device’s advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. AppLovin’s privacy policy is at applovin.com/privacy.

Notifications and support

Reminders, like the full-lives alert, are scheduled on your phone and don’t send data anywhere. A debug log is only shared if you choose Send Debug Log in Help & Support, and only through the app you pick.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to AbdulQRS@outlook.com.
